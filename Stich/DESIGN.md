## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of thoughtful everyday essentials. Inspired by the understated discipline of Uniqlo U, the architectural purity of COS, and the transparent craft of Everlane, the interface projects calm confidence, tactile quality, and effortless utility. The audience seeks elevated wardrobe essentials—cotton basics stripped of unnecessary ornamentation, prioritizing fiber density, cut precision, and durability.

The visual direction is **Minimalism with Warm Tactile Editorial Nuance**. Generous breathing room, structural alignment, subtle micro-borders, and pristine typographic balance anchor the experience. While the brand storytelling remains serene and organic, high-converting commercial touchpoints are decisively accentuated to facilitate a seamless transition into marketplace checkout flows.

## Colors

The palette establishes a strict separation between brand storytelling and commercial conversion:

- **Foundation & Surfaces:** The primary canvas uses `#FBFBF9` (warm neutral off-white), rejecting clinical digital white for organic paper-like warmth. Secondary card panels, size matrices, and module backgrounds use `#F4F3EF` to create soft tonal contrast without reliance on shadows.
- **Brand Identity:** `#5F6F52` (signature sage green) and its shade `#4A5840` serve as the brand mark anchor, representing organic cotton integrity, fabric badges, and editorial highlights. Soft tint `#EBEFE8` provides background fills for feature pills.
- **Typography & Structure:** `#18181B` and `#27272A` deliver deep, high-contrast legibility for display titles, specs, and architectural hairline dividers. `#71717A` handles secondary garment details and dimension sub-labels.
- **Conversion Accent:** `#EE4D2D` (Shopee Orange) is reserved exclusively for high-intent conversion actions (e.g., direct Shopee checkout buttons, persistent bottom conversion rails, and limited promotional tags). It must never be diluted on non-transactional elements.

## Typography

Typographic scale and balance drive the editorial nature of this design system. Plus Jakarta Sans provides clean geometric clarity with subtle warmth:

- **Hero & Headings:** Use tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) with moderate weights (`500` to `600`) to evoke contemporary fashion editorial spreads rather than aggressive tech bolding.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrows:** Category headers, fabric weights (e.g., "COTTON COMBED 24S / 30S"), and top announcement text use `label-caps` set in uppercase with tracked spacing (`0.12em`).
- **Body & Garment Specs:** Fabric education requires high line heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) to guarantee reading comfort across mobile and desktop viewports.
- **Size Tables & Measurements:** Render numbers and unit dimensions in tabular-optimized regular weights for clean vertical alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs an intentional 12-column editorial grid on desktop (max width: `1280px` centered) and a dynamic 4-column layout on mobile devices:

- **Rhythm & White Space:** Spacing between sections scales up to `space-3xl` (`96px`), creating deliberate pauses that showcase cotton drape, ribbing detail, and silhouette lines without visual congestion.
- **Catalog & Comparison Layout:** Product cards sit on a 3-column desktop grid collapsing into a 1-column or 2-column swipeable matrix on mobile. The fabric comparison matrix (24s vs 30s) uses a balanced 50/50 dual card split.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop`: `>= 1024px` (Full 12-column split, persistent contextual sticky headers).
  - `Tablet`: `768px – 1023px` (8-column grid, horizontal side-scrolling swatch modules).
  - `Mobile`: `< 768px` (4-column grid, edge margins compressed to `1.25rem`, fixed bottom CTA bar visible).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained entirely through **tonal layering and low-contrast hairline boundaries** rather than traditional multi-stage drop shadows:

- **Base Surfaces:** Flat `#FBFBF9` base canvas.
- **Card & Spec Modules:** Inset against base canvas using surface-subtle `#F4F3EF` with a 1px border colored at `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`.
- **Floating Overlays:** The persistent bottom CTA bar and interactive swatch preview drawers utilize frosted glass layering (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) combined with background `rgba(251, 251, 249, 0.88)` and a microscopic upward border `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`.
- **Shadow Tokens:** Heavy drop shadows are omitted. Only the floating Shopee conversion bar uses an ambient, tinted ground shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The system uses a **Soft (Level 1)** corner philosophy. Subtle curvature reflects the tailored precision of garment cutting patterns without softening into consumer-app bubble aesthetics:

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border radius maintains architectural crispness.
- **Product & Macro Detail Cards:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) border radius on image wrappers and technical comparison panels.
- **Category & Feature Badges:** Pill-shaped full radius (`9999px`) for trust indicators ("100% Cotton Combed", "Pre-Shrunk", "Garansi Retur 100%"), creating deliberate visual contrast against rectangular product imagery.
- **Color Swatches:** Perfect circles (`24px` × `24px`) with a `2px` spacing ring when active.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Shopee Conversion CTA (Primary):** Solid `#EE4D2D` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, semi-bold typography. Features an integrated marketplace bag icon. Hover state transitions to `#D83E1F`. Height: `48px` (desktop), `52px` (mobile touch target).
- **Secondary Brand Action:** Solid `#18181B` or outline `#18181B` (`1px` border), transparent background, `#18181B` text. Used for sizing guide toggles and catalog filters.
- **Fabric Switcher (24s / 30s):** Segmented pill toggle control. Active weight highlighted with `#5F6F52` text and a subtle `#FFFFFF` pill card elevation; inactive weight rendered in `#71717A`.

### Product Display Cards
- **Structure:** Frameless image container with aspect ratio `3:4` or `4:5`. Background `#F4F3EF` under garments.
- **Information Hierarchy:**
  1. Product name in `headline-sm` (`#18181B`).
  2. Fabric spec chip ("Cotton Combed 24s").
  3. Interactive color swatch dots (Solid Black, Dark Grey, Clean White) triggering image change.
  4. Price line with integrated direct Shopee checkout micro-button.

### Color Swatch Selector
- **Tokens:** Circle tokens (`size: 24px`). Selected state adds a `1px` outer ring in `#18181B` separated by a `2px` white space offset.

### Size Chart & Detail Matrix
- **Table Styling:** Minimalist horizontal hairline dividers (`1px solid rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`).
- **Cells:** Header row set in `label-caps` (`#71717A`), content cells in `body-sm` (`#18181B`). Alternating subtle highlights for recommended fit ranges based on height/weight metrics.

### Trust Badges & Guarantee Pills
- **Styling:** Height `28px`, full rounded pill, background `#EBEFE8`, text `#4A5840`, font `label-sm` with a micro checkmark or leaf icon.

### Sticky Marketplace Conversion Rail
- **Placement:** Affixed to viewport bottom on mobile (`z-index: 50`).
- **Composition:** Split view featuring selected variant summary (e.g., "24s Solid Black - L") on the left, and a full-width high-contrast orange Shopee button on the right.
